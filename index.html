DEPA-Net · Stereo Super-Resolution

Geometry-aware reconstruction from stereo pairs

  • Proposed DEPA-Net, combining deformable epipolar paired-attention, disparity-aware occlusion fusion with confidence estimation, and a multi-phase training curriculum.
  • Implemented dense cross-view interaction via interleaved SCAM modules and bidirectional DEPA at every encoder scale.
  • Added a correlation-prior bottleneck and frequency-aware refinement branch for high-frequency detail recovery.

Causal Counterfactuals for ECG Anomalies

Explainable time-series anomaly detection
